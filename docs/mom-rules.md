# MOM work permit preparation

The app prepares a checklist, reminders, and a PDF for one staff-approved match. It does not submit anything to the Ministry of Manpower. Work Permit applications are lodged by a person on [WP Online](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker) with Singpass or CorpPass.

Every numeric rule lives in `config/mom-rules.ts` and can be overridden with the `MOM_*` variables in `.env.example`. The file is flagged **verify against current MOM guidance**. Each rule carries `verifiedOn: 2026-11-28` and its source URL. The values were transcribed on 2026-11-26 and checked again on 2026-11-28 from the public pages below. MOM can change them. Operators should re-read those pages before using a pack in a real placement. An environment override does not make a number official. New money figures are integer cents.

## Sources

- [Migrant domestic worker eligibility](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/fdw-eligibility) — a new application is age 23 to below 50. A helper aged 50 or older may renew the Work Permit until below 60. Also female, at least eight years of formal education, and the approved source countries and regions. Matching still treats age 50 as ineligible for a new introduction.
- [Employer requirements](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/employer-requirements) — employer at least 21 and not an undischarged bankrupt. Caregiving needs and financial ability are described qualitatively, so this product does not invent an income number.
- [Employers' Orientation Programme](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/employers-orientation-programme-eop) — first-time employers, configured as two working days before submission. The completion day is not counted.
- [Security bond](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/security-bond) — SGD 5,000, with Malaysia on the configured exemption list.
- [Insurance requirements](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/insurance-requirements) — medical insurance and personal accident cover, each configured at a minimum of SGD 60,000 a year. Inpatient and day surgery each need their own sub-limit of at least that amount (stored as 6000000 cents). From 1 July 2023, claims above S$15,000 are 75% insurer and 25% employer.
- [Settling-in Programme](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/settling-in-programme-sip) — first-time helpers, within seven days of arrival excluding Sunday and public holidays. SIP cannot be attended on the arrival day itself. A pack that records the same day is refused.
- [Medical examination](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/medical-examination) — within 14 days of arrival.
- [Six-monthly medical examination](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/six-monthly-medical-examination) — every 6 months during employment. Helpers aged 50 and older are exempt and only need an examination when the permit is renewed.
- [Levy](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/foreign-domestic-worker-levy/paying-levy) — S$300 a month for the first helper (S$9.87 a day), S$450 for each subsequent helper (S$14.80 a day), concessionary S$60 (S$1.98 a day), concession capped at 2 helpers. For a first-time helper the levy starts on the 5th day counting the arrival date. Otherwise it starts the day after arrival. Pay by the 17th of the following month. Stored as integer cents.
- [Rest days and well-being](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/employers-guide/rest-days-and-well-being) — one rest day a week. If she agrees to work it, pay at least one extra day's salary. At least one rest day a month cannot be compensated away (a full day, or two half days), and that day may be deferred by up to one month. The staff "rest days per month" field must be at least that minimum.
- [Agency fee FAQ](https://www.mom.gov.sg/faq/employment-agencies/how-will-foreign-workers-know-the-amount-that-they-are-expected-to-pay-the-singapore-ea) and [Agency fee: Know the Limits](https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/publications/foreign-workers/agency-fee-know-the-limits.pdf) — the fee charged to the worker is at most one month of fixed salary for each year of the work pass or the contract, whichever is shorter, and not more than two months. If the employer terminates within 6 months, refund 50% of the agency fee collected. A billing record above the cap is refused and is not stored.
- [Work Permit hub](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker)

`publicHolidays` is filled from the MOM public holidays page for 2025, 2026, and 2027 (`config/public-holidays.ts`, last verified 2026-11-26). Each year records that source URL. A Monday is included when MOM says it is a holiday if the rest day falls on the Sunday holiday. 2027 lunar dates (Chinese New Year, Hari Raya Puasa, Hari Raya Haji, Vesak Day, and Deepavali) can move when the holidays are gazetted. Re-check them before relying on 2027. Staff can add or suppress a date under Holidays. `MOM_PUBLIC_HOLIDAYS` still replaces the whole list when it is set.

SIP dates exclude Sunday and those holidays and count Saturday. That is separate from the Employers' Orientation Programme, which uses working days and skips Saturday as well. Medical examination dates are calendar days. Permit renewal lead time (`REMINDER_PERMIT_LEAD_DAYS`, default 30) and the 30-day rest-day follow-up are agency windows, not MOM figures.

The rules review page lists every catalog value with its unit, MOM link, last verified date, and who verified it. Nothing on that page invents a new figure. A row needs verification until an admin marks it. Rows older than `MOM_RULE_STALE_DAYS` (default 90) are also flagged on the staff desk.

The PDF states that it is a preparation pack, not a lodged application.
